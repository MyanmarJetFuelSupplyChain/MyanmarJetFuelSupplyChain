Myanma Oil and Gas Enterprise (MOGE)
Profile 9.2
Summary Assessment
Myanma Oil and Gas Enterprise (MOGE) is the junta-controlled state enterprise that holds exclusive rights to Myanmar's oil and gas reserves and sits at the centre of both the domestic crude-to-aviation-fuel chain and the junta's primary source of foreign revenue. As the mandatory purchaser of all crude oil from Goldpetrol, MOGE receives the oil, transfers it to MPE for refining at Mann Thanbayarkan, where it becomes aviation fuel and diesel that primarily supply military bases. Separately, MOGE is the junta's largest single source of foreign income, earning at least USD 55 million per month in Thai Baht gas revenues as of end of 2023. The EU designated MOGE on 21 February 2022. The US issued a financial services directive on 31 October 2023 prohibiting US persons from providing financial services to MOGE, short of a full asset-blocking designation. Switzerland designated MOGE on 11 March 2025. The UK, Canada, and Australia have not designated MOGE as of April 2026. The UN Special Rapporteur explicitly recommended in June 2024 that the US upgrade its directive to full sanctions and that the UN Security Council freeze MOGE's assets.

1
Role in the Domestic Crude-to-Aviation-Fuel Chain

Under Goldpetrol's IPRCs, 39 percent of total production is delivered directly to MOGE in crude oil, with further oil reaching MOGE through taxes, royalties, and domestic market obligations. MOGE receives more than half of all oil extracted before Goldpetrol receives any cash. MOGE transfers this crude to MPE for refining at Mann Thanbayarkan. A source with direct supply chain knowledge confirmed to JFM that Mann's petroleum products, including aviation turbine fuel, primarily supply military bases in Magway Region. Leaked documents documented military trucks transporting Jet A-1 from Mann to air bases including Hmawbi, Meikhtila, Nay Pyi Taw, Taungoo, and Monywa. MPE's own website lists aviation turbine fuel as a current Mann product. (JFM Jan 2025; AI Nov 2022)

MOGE is also the junta's primary foreign currency earner. It receives gas revenues from the Yadana, Yetagun, and Shwe pipelines supplying Thailand and China, earning at least USD 55 million per month in Thai Baht payments from the Yadana field alone as of end of 2023. These revenues are processed through MEB following the US financial services directive, which effectively excluded MOGE from USD-denominated banking. The EU's February 2022 designation prompted Bank of China to reportedly stop transmitting euro payments to MOGE for Shwe gas revenues, placing over USD 500 million into escrow outside the junta's reach. (UNSR Jun 2024; JFM Feb 2025)

The European Union designated MOGE on 21 February 2022, finding that MOGE contributes to the Myanmar military's capabilities to carry out activities undermining democracy and the rule of law in Myanmar. Switzerland designated MOGE under its SECO sanctions list on 11 March 2025, following a sustained advocacy campaign by Justice For Myanmar including a freedom of information request to SECO in March 2024 and reporting by Tages-Anzeiger. The United States issued a financial services directive against MOGE on 31 October 2023, taking effect on 15 December 2023. The directive prohibits US persons from providing financial services to or for the benefit of MOGE or its property or interests in property; it is a sectoral measure that places MOGE on the US OFAC Consolidated Non-SDN list rather than the Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons list. The United Kingdom, Canada and Australia had not designated MOGE as of April 2026. (JFM Jan 2025; JFM Feb 2025; UNSR Jun 2024)

The UN Special Rapporteur's June 2024 report explicitly recommended that the United States upgrade its financial services directive to full sanctions on MOGE, and called on the UN Security Council to sanction MOGE and freeze assets that rightfully belong to the people of Myanmar, including billions in foreign currency reserves. The UNSR also noted that under current UK and EU law, all Myanmar state-owned enterprises should be considered sanctioned given EU and UK sanctions on the SAC itself, but that both jurisdictions have failed to treat MOGE as fully sanctioned in practice. The UNSR described this as a significant oversight creating confusion for financial institutions. (UNSR Jun 2024)

The EU designation had an immediate practical consequence: Bank of China reportedly stopped transmitting euro-denominated payments to MOGE for gas exports from the Shwe gas field following the February 2022 designation. Payments reportedly totalling over USD 500 million were placed into escrow accounts and remained outside the junta's reach as of the UNSR's writing in June 2024. This represents a direct financial cost to the junta from the EU designation. (UNSR Jun 2024)

3
Sanctions Evasion

Following the October 2023 US financial services directive, the SAC's energy ministry instructed MEB to open accounts for MOGE revenues under the name "Kant Kaw" rather than MOGE, according to a leaked letter to the Central Bank of Myanmar cited by the UNSR. This manoeuvre was documented in the UNSR's June 2024 report and illustrates the junta's active circumvention of financial restrictions through account renaming. JFM has also flagged the risk that MOGE's outgoing payments to joint venture partners, previously processed through MFTB, may have shifted to MEB following MFTB's June 2023 US designation. (UNSR Jun 2024; JFM Jan 2025)

Key Relationships
•Goldpetrol Joint Operating Company Inc. / Interra Resources Ltd (Profile 9.1): Goldpetrol's sole contractual counterparty under its IPRCs. All crude oil extracted by Goldpetrol from Chauk and Yenangyaung is sold exclusively to MOGE. MOGE paid Goldpetrol more than USD 11 million through MFTB between February and May 2022. Current payment bank is undisclosed. (JFM Jan 2025; JFM Feb 2025)
•Myanma Petrochemical Enterprise (MPE): The downstream state enterprise to which MOGE transfers crude oil for refining at Mann Thanbayarkan. MPE processes Goldpetrol's crude into aviation fuel, diesel and other petroleum products that primarily supply military bases in Magway Region. MPE has not been sanctioned by any government as of April 2026. (JFM Jan 2025; JFM May 2025)
•Myanma Economic Bank (MEB) (Profile 8.3): The state bank through which MOGE channels its foreign gas revenues. MEB processed approximately USD 165 million in Thai Baht revenues on behalf of MOGE during Q3 FY2023 (approximately USD 55 million per month). The SAC's energy ministry attempted to route these revenues under the alias "Kant Kaw" to evade the US financial services directive. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•Myanma Foreign Trade Bank (MFTB) (Profile 8.4): Used by MOGE to pay Goldpetrol more than USD 11 million between February and May 2022. The US sanctioned MFTB in June 2023, prompting MOGE to shift Goldpetrol payments to an undisclosed bank. MFTB is designated by the US, Canada and Australia. (JFM Jan 2025)
•Ministry of Electricity and Energy: MOGE operates as an operational department of the Ministry of Electricity and Energy, which the junta seized control of following the February 2021 coup by appointing new ministers and managing directors. MOGE is described as functioning as an operational department of the ministry rather than as an autonomous corporate entity. (AI Nov 2022; JFM Jan 2025)

Advocacy Interventions to Date
•21 February 2022. The EU designates MOGE, the first major jurisdiction to do so. Bank of China reportedly stops transmitting euro payments for Shwe gas revenues, placing over USD 500 million into escrow. (JFM Feb 2025; UNSR Jun 2024)
•July 2022. PTTEP, the Thai state oil company, terminates a gas exploration contract with MOGE for the M3 block. (JFM Feb 2025)
•31 October 2023. The US issues a financial services directive prohibiting US persons from providing financial services to MOGE, effective 15 December 2023. This falls short of a full asset-blocking designation. The SAC responds with the Kant Kaw evasion attempt. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•26 June 2024. The UNSR's Banking on the Death Trade report calls on the US to upgrade the directive to full sanctions and on the UN Security Council to freeze MOGE's assets. The UNSR explicitly identifies MOGE as the junta's "single-biggest source of foreign revenue." No upgrade follows. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•11 March 2025. Switzerland designates MOGE under its SECO sanctions list. (JFM Feb 2025)
•April 2026. The UK, Canada, and Australia have not designated MOGE. The SAC rebranded as the State Security and Peace Commission (SSPC) on 31 July 2025, creating legal uncertainty about whether the UK's existing SAC designation continues to cover MOGE derivatively. (JFM Feb 2025)
6
Recommended Actions
•Designate MOGE in the UK, Canada, and Australia. The EU, US, and Switzerland have all acted; the remaining three have not. The UK's SAC designation previously provided derivative coverage, but the SAC's rebrand as the SSPC on 31 July 2025 creates legal uncertainty that only an explicit MOGE designation can resolve. For Canada and Australia, the MFTB and MICB designation precedent and the UNSR June 2024 report provide the evidentiary basis. (UNSR Jun 2024; JFM Feb 2025)
•Press the US to upgrade the financial services directive to a full asset-blocking designation. The UNSR explicitly called for this in June 2024. A full SDN designation would extend restrictions beyond US persons to any entity transacting with MOGE through the US financial system, creating far greater practical disruption to MOGE's gas revenues and procurement payments than the current directive. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•Call on the UN Security Council to freeze MOGE's assets. The UNSR explicitly recommended this in June 2024. A UNSC freeze would bind all member states and close the jurisdictional gaps that currently allow the UK, Canada, and Australia to remain outside the designation coalition. Civil society organisations accredited to the UNSC Myanmar file should formally support the UNSR's recommendation and engage Security Council members. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•Investigate whether MOGE outgoing payments to joint venture partners have shifted to MEB. JFM flagged this risk explicitly. If MOGE has been using MEB to pay joint venture partners including Goldpetrol since MFTB's June 2023 designation, those payment flows may now be subject to Canada's MEB designation. Financial intelligence units and investigative journalists with access to Thai and Myanmar banking data should investigate. (JFM Jan 2025; UNSR Jun 2024)
