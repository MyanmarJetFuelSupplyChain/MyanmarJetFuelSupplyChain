NOBLE (formerly ASTRA / TRAA3 / HURON)
Profile 6.6
Summary Assessment
NOBLE (IMO 9162928), formerly known as ASTRA and before that TRAA3 and HURON, is a Guinea-flagged chemical/oil products tanker that made four confirmed deliveries of aviation fuel to Myanmar's military junta between January and June 2025. NOBLE is the larger of two sister vessels in the Myanmar aviation fuel ghost fleet, with REEF (IMO 9263382, Profile 6.5) as its companion. Both share Sea Route Ship Management FZE as ISM and commercial manager and were designated by OFAC on the same date. Amnesty International's January 2026 investigation established that NOBLE loitered at the Fujairah Offshore Anchorage Area (FOAA) before each delivery, turned off its AIS transponder during offloading at the Myan Oil Terminal, and that satellite imagery confirmed an Iranian loading point at Bandar Abbas for at least the June 2025 delivery. No cargo volumes have been confirmed in any reviewed source for any of NOBLE's four deliveries. The United States designated NOBLE (then named ASTRA, callsign TRAA3) on 27 June 2024 under the Iran Sanctions program (Executive Order 13846) for its history of transporting Iranian petrochemical products, before it made any of its documented Myanmar deliveries. The UK, EU, Canada and Australia have not designated NOBLE. Justice For Myanmar called for coordinated designation across all five jurisdictions in February 2026. Sea Hill Maritime SA (UAE, Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah) is the registered owner. Sea Route Ship Management FZE (UAE, Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah) is the ISM and commercial manager, itself also US-sanctioned since 27 June 2024. As of late January 2026, SynMax Intelligence data showed NOBLE feigning an anchorage position off southern Iraq while in reality loitering near Bandar Abbas, indicating preparation for a further delivery at the time of reporting.

2
Iranian Fuel Origin and AIS Evasion

All four of NOBLE's Myanmar deliveries are attributed to Iranian fuel sources. Kpler, a commodity intelligence platform that tracks global fuel flows using AIS data, satellite imagery, customs information and other sources, assessed all of NOBLE's Myanmar shipments as originating from Iran. For at least one delivery (the June 2025 delivery, delivery 4 above), Amnesty International's satellite imagery analysis placed NOBLE at Bandar Abbas, Iran, after it had staged at the FOAA anchorage area in Fujairah, UAE, and before it arrived in Myanmar. The use of FOAA as a staging point, followed by an Iranian loading stop before final delivery, is consistent with the supply pattern documented for REEF. (AI Jan 2026)

NOBLE turned off its AIS radar during offloading at the Myan Oil Terminal in Yangon. This AIS deactivation is documented by Amnesty International's January 2026 investigation as one of the defining characteristics of the ghost fleet supply chain: vessels go dark during sensitive port calls to prevent real-time detection and to complicate post-hoc verification of port calls. AIS data and satellite imagery together indicate that NOBLE loitered at the FOAA anchorage before each of its documented Myanmar deliveries, using the offshore bunkering and ship-to-ship transfer area as a staging or transfer point consistent with shadow fleet methodology. (AI Jan 2026)

Reuters' concurrent January 2026 investigation reported that as of late January 2026, SynMax Intelligence data showed NOBLE feigning an anchorage position off the southern tip of Iraq while in reality loitering near the Iranian port of Bandar Abbas, waiting to set sail. This indicates that at the time of the reporting, NOBLE was actively preparing for a further delivery beyond the four confirmed Myanmar deliveries. (Reuters Jan 2026)

3
Prior Sanctions History and Iran Shadow Fleet Context

NOBLE was designated by OFAC on 27 June 2024 under the Iran Sanctions program (Executive Order 13846), while operating under the name ASTRA and flagged to Gabon. The designation linked ASTRA to Sea Route Ship Management FZE, which was simultaneously designated for the same Iran sanctions violations. The designation was made on the basis of the vessel's history of transporting sanctioned fuel from Iran, before it made any of its documented Myanmar aviation fuel deliveries: all four Myanmar deliveries occurred in 2025, at least six months after the OFAC designation. (AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)

NOBLE is part of Iran's shadow fleet network and operates in close operational parallel with REEF (IMO 9263382, Profile 6.5). Reuters described NOBLE as REEF's "larger sister ship." Both vessels share Sea Route Ship Management FZE as ISM and commercial manager and were simultaneously designated by OFAC on 27 June 2024. The IRGC is documented as dominating the fuel-smuggling networks of which NOBLE forms a part. The continued Myanmar deliveries by a US-sanctioned vessel across all four documented deliveries in 2025 demonstrate both the limits of vessel-level US designation without complementary measures in other jurisdictions, and the capacity of the ghost fleet to sustain multiple coordinated delivery cycles simultaneously. (Reuters Jan 2026; AI Jan 2026)

7
Recommended Actions
•Urgently designate NOBLE across the UK, EU, Canada and Australia: NOBLE and REEF are the only two vessels in the Myanmar aviation fuel ghost fleet to have been designated by any government, yet both continued to make Myanmar deliveries after their OFAC designations in June 2024. NOBLE made four documented Myanmar deliveries across all of 2025, and as of late January 2026 was actively preparing a further delivery. This demonstrates that vessel-level US designation alone is insufficient without matching designations across other major jurisdictions. Without UK, EU, Canadian and Australian designations, the US sanction is easily evaded through flag changes, operator changes and continued access to non-US financial, insurance and port services. JFM's February 2026 call for coordinated designation across all four remaining jurisdictions remains unimplemented. (JFM Feb 2026; AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
•Designate Sea Hill Maritime SA and Sea Route Ship Management FZE across all jurisdictions where not already designated: OFAC designated Sea Route Ship Management FZE on 27 June 2024 alongside NOBLE and REEF, but Sea Hill Maritime SA (the registered owner of NOBLE) has not been designated by any government. JFM's February 2026 call covers both entities. The common Hamriyah Free Zone address shared by Sea Hill Maritime SA and Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA (the owner of REEF) suggests a common beneficial owner or operator behind both vessels, and any future designation action should assess whether the two owner entities are connected. (JFM Feb 2026)
•Engage Guinea's maritime registry on NOBLE's flag registration: NOBLE is currently Guinea-flagged, having reflagged from Gabon following its OFAC designation. The Guinea flag registry should be formally notified of NOBLE's documented history as a US-sanctioned vessel delivering aviation fuel to Myanmar. Guinea is a member of the International Maritime Organization and is subject to the obligations of SOLAS and other IMO conventions. Formal notification of flag authorities has previously prompted registry action against shadow fleet vessels in other sanctions-evasion contexts. (AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
•Engage UAE authorities regarding Sea Route Ship Management FZE, Sea Hill Maritime SA and Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA, all domiciled in the Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah: The UAE holds domestic jurisdiction over the ISM and commercial manager of both NOBLE and REEF (Sea Route Ship Management FZE) and the registered owners of both vessels (Sea Hill Maritime SA for NOBLE; Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA for REEF). All three entities share a Hamriyah Free Zone address. UAE engagement through the Hamriyah Free Zone Authority and the UAE Executive Office of Anti-Money Laundering and Counter Terrorism Financing could complement multilateral designation efforts. The UAE's 2025 AML Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025), in force since 14 October 2025, expanded sanctions-evasion offences and lowered the evidentiary threshold for establishing AML liability, providing stronger domestic legal grounds for action. (JFM Feb 2026; AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
•Pursue diplomatic engagement with Iran on IRGC-linked fuel exports to Myanmar: Satellite imagery confirmed that NOBLE loaded fuel at Iran's Bandar Abbas port prior to its June 2025 Myanmar delivery, approximately 8 kilometres from the Bandar Abbas Oil Refinery overseen by NIORDC, a subsidiary of NIOC (which OFAC identified as an agent or affiliate of the IRGC in 2012) and designated by OFAC since October 2020. Kpler commodity intelligence assessed all four NOBLE deliveries as sourced from Iran. UN Special Rapporteur Tom Andrews explicitly called for the Iranian government to be held accountable following Reuters' January 2026 investigation. Governments imposing Iran sanctions and engaging Iran bilaterally should include Myanmar aviation fuel exports as a specific point of engagement, alongside existing Iran sanctions pressure on IRGC-linked entities. (Reuters Jan 2026; AI Jan 2026)
•Monitor NOBLE (IMO 9162928) for further Myanmar-bound voyages and update designation submissions accordingly: As of late January 2026, SynMax Intelligence data confirmed NOBLE loitering near Bandar Abbas while feigning a position off southern Iraq, indicating active preparation for a further delivery beyond the four confirmed Myanmar deliveries. Any additional confirmed delivery constitutes material evidence of ongoing supply chain activity and should be added to designation submissions across all five jurisdictions. Monitoring should extend to REEF (IMO 9263382, Profile 6.5), which was similarly documented as loading and heading toward Yangon as of late January 2026. The IMO number 9162928 must be specified in all monitoring and screening activities to avoid confusion with the separate vessel NOBLE (IMO 9333400), which is sanctioned under Russian oil sanctions programmes. (Reuters Jan 2026)
