Myan-Oil Company Limited & Rich Ray Trading Company Limited
Profile 1.6
Summary Assessment
Myan-Oil Company Limited and Rich Ray Trading Company Limited are Myanmar-registered companies documented by the UN Special Rapporteur as Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers of aviation fuel in calendar year 2023. Both operated through United Amara Bank. Myan-Oil purchased from CB Energy, a Thailand-registered trading company, with payments received at Krung Thai Bank. Rich Ray Trading purchased from Tawan-Oil Trading, a second Thailand-registered company, with payments received at Kasikorn Bank. Together with Swan Energy, the three Asia Sun Group proxies collectively paid approximately USD 15 million for their purchases; the UNSR's documentation does not provide a per-entity breakdown. No corporate registry information, ownership details or individual directors have been identified for either company in any source reviewed. Both were sanctioned by the United Kingdom in October 2024, the only jurisdiction to have done so. The United States, European Union, Canada and Australia have not designated either entity.

1
Role in the Aviation Fuel Supply Chain

Myan-Oil Company Limited and Rich Ray Trading Company Limited are Myanmar-registered companies that functioned as proxy purchasers of aviation fuel on behalf of Asia Sun Group in calendar year 2023. Both are distinct from NEPAS and its own proxy structure, which operated a parallel purchasing chain independently through its own proxy, Intraco (International Green Agrotrade Company Limited; see Profile 1.2). Both Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading form part of the Asia Sun Group / Shoon Group network, whose role as the junta's private-sector partner for aviation fuel import and distribution is documented from before the February 2021 coup, though neither Myan-Oil nor Rich Ray Trading appears in any source prior to the UNSR's 2023 trade records.

Myan-Oil Company Limited should not be confused with Myan Oil Terminal Company Limited (formerly Puma Energy Asia Sun Company Limited), which is the physical aviation fuel terminal at Thilawa port owned by P.E.I. Energy Pte. Ltd. and covered in Profile 1.3. The similar names reflect the Shoon Group's use of related naming across its Myanmar corporate structure.

2023 purchasing role

The UN Special Rapporteur's June 2024 Banking on the Death Trade report documented, from confidential trade records, that Asia Sun Group used three Myanmar-registered proxy companies to purchase aviation fuel in calendar year 2023: Swan Energy, Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading. Myan-Oil purchased from CB Energy, a Thailand-registered trading company, with payments processed through United Amara Bank in Myanmar to CB Energy's account at Krung Thai Bank in Thailand. Rich Ray Trading purchased from Tawan-Oil Trading, a second Thailand-registered company, with payments processed through United Amara Bank to Tawan-Oil's account at Kasikorn Bank in Thailand. The three proxies collectively paid approximately USD 15 million. No per-entity breakdown is given in the UNSR's documentation. The UK sanctions list records Myan-Oil and Swan Energy at the same registered address on Bergayar Road, Sanchuang Township, Yangon, and records the same email address, Office.myanmar@swanenergy.co, for both entities, confirming their operational co-location. (UNSR Jun 2024; UK Sanctions List MYA0081, MYA0085)

Source limitation: Beyond their role as proxy purchasers in the UNSR's 2023 transaction table and the data on the UK sanctions list entries (MYA0081 and MYA0084), no additional information on Myan-Oil or Rich Ray Trading has been identified in any reviewed source. No beneficial ownership, directors, or operational history prior to 2023 is available from any source. Note: Intraco / International Green Agrotrade Company Limited (registration number 138705277), which is assigned to this joint profile number in the database outline, is a NEPAS proxy rather than an Asia Sun Group proxy and is documented in Profile 1.2.

Key Relationships
•Asia Sun Group Company Limited / Shoon Group: The parent network under which Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading operated as proxy purchasers. The UK designated both companies as associated with Asia Sun Trading Company Limited. Asia Sun Group is sanctioned in all five jurisdictions. See Profile 1.4. (UNSR Jun 2024; UK Oct 2024)
•CB Energy (Thailand): Thailand-registered trading company from which Myan-Oil purchased aviation fuel in 2023, with payment processed through United Amara Bank to CB Energy's account at Krung Thai Bank. CB Energy is unsanctioned in all jurisdictions. See Profile 3.1. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•Tawan-Oil Trading (Thailand): Thailand-registered trading company from which Rich Ray Trading purchased aviation fuel in 2023, with payment processed through United Amara Bank to Tawan-Oil's account at Kasikorn Bank. Tawan-Oil Trading is unsanctioned in all jurisdictions. See Profile 3.2. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•United Amara Bank (UAB): The Myanmar private bank through which both Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading processed their aviation fuel purchase payments in 2023. The UN Special Rapporteur explicitly calls for UAB's international banking relationships to be frozen. Unsanctioned in all jurisdictions. See Profile 8.1. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•Swan Energy Company Limited: The third Asia Sun Group proxy purchaser documented alongside Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading in the UNSR's 2023 transaction table. Swan Energy and Myan-Oil both purchased from CB Energy via Krung Thai Bank. The UK sanctions list records Myan-Oil and Swan Energy at the same registered address and with the same contact email (Office.myanmar@swanenergy.co), confirming operational co-location. Swan Energy is sanctioned by the UK and Canada. See Profile 1.5. (UNSR Jun 2024; UK Sanctions List MYA0081, MYA0085; JFM Oct 2024)

4
Advocacy Interventions to Date
•June 2024. The UN Special Rapporteur's Banking on the Death Trade report publicly identifies Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading for the first time, documenting both as Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers in the 2023 supply chain. The UNSR's Figure 9 records both as unsanctioned in all jurisdictions at the time of publication and notes that no country has holistically sanctioned the Asia Sun network. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•October 2024. The United Kingdom becomes the first and, as of April 2026, only jurisdiction to sanction Myan-Oil Company Limited and Rich Ray Trading Company Limited, designating both as associated with Asia Sun Trading Company Limited. Justice for Myanmar welcomes the designations, noting the UK is the first jurisdiction to act on either entity. (UK Oct 2024; JFM Oct 2024)
•February 2026. Justice for Myanmar calls for coordinated sanctions on the aviation fuel supply chain, including vessels, their owners and managers, Hai Linh and MPE. Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading are not named explicitly in this press release. Both remain sanctioned only by the UK as of April 2026. (JFM Feb 2026)
5
Recommended Actions
•Designate Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading in the US, EU, Canada and Australia: Both entities are documented in the UNSR's 2023 transaction table as direct purchasers of aviation fuel operating under Asia Sun Group. Both fall within the August 2023 OFAC jet fuel sector determination, which covers any entity operating in the jet fuel sector of the Burmese economy. For Myan-Oil specifically, the co-location with Canada-sanctioned Swan Energy at the same registered address and email (Office.myanmar@swanenergy.co) provides additional evidentiary support for its operational integration within the same network. Canada sanctioned Swan Energy in the same October 2024 round in which the UK sanctioned Myan-Oil; the same evidence base supports a Canadian designation of Myan-Oil. The UK acted in October 2024; coordinated designation by the remaining four jurisdictions would close that gap and remove the evasion opportunity created by single-jurisdiction action. The UNSR's Figure 7 transaction documentation provides the primary evidentiary basis for OFAC, EU EEAS, Canadian Global Affairs and Australian DFAT submissions. (UNSR Jun 2024; OFAC Aug 2023; UK Sanctions List MYA0081, MYA0085)
•Engage CB Energy and Tawan-Oil Trading on continued exposure: CB Energy and Tawan-Oil Trading are the direct Thai commercial counterparties for Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading respectively. Both remain unsanctioned in all jurisdictions. Civil society should formally notify both companies of the UK designations of their Myanmar counterparties and of the UNSR's documentation of their role in the 2023 supply chain, requesting confirmation that all transactions with Asia Sun Group network entities have ceased. The OFAC jet fuel sector determination provides the legal framework for communicating the risk of US designation to both companies. (UNSR Jun 2024; OFAC Aug 2023)
