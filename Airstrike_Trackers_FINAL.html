IISS Myanmar Conflict Map

The Myanmar Conflict Map is an interactive dashboard developed by the International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS) that visualizes Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project (ACLED) data adapted to the Myanmar context. Users can map reports of violence across six distinct warscapes and access accompanying in-depth explainers on key conflict dynamics.

Filter categories

  • Event type (attack/armed clash, remote explosives/IEDs, air/drone strike, crackdowns, infrastructure destruction)
  • Date
  • Actor (State Administration Council forces, anti-SAC forces, ethnic armed organizations, civilians)
  • Warscape/geographic region

The dashboard does not provide individual casualty details (names, ages, etc.).

Website https://myanmar.iiss.org/dashboard

NUG Aerial Attacks by the Military Junta Database

The National Unity Government (NUG) Human Rights Ministry's "Aerial Attacks by the Military Junta" database tracks aerial attacks since the coup. The dashboard displays total cases, civilians killed, civilians injured, and infrastructure destroyed.

Filter categories

  • Year
  • Month
  • Attack type (airstrike, drone, paramotor, gyrocopter)
  • Severity (dead or injured)
  • Age (under 18, above 18, unknown)
  • Destroyed building (medical, school, religious building)

The database provides aggregate figures but does not provide individual details (names, specific type of religious building, etc.).

Website https://mohr.nugmyanmar.org/en/data-dashboards/aerialattacksbymilitaryjunta/

Myanmar Peace Monitor Military Junta's Airstrikes in Myanmar Dashboard

The Myanmar Peace Monitor Military Junta's Airstrikes in Myanmar Dashboard tracks airstrikes since 1 February 2021. Published by Burma News International (BNI), the platform displays total airstrike events, civilian deaths, and total People's Defence Force (PDF) deaths.

Filter categories

  • Actor (ABSDF, BGF, Civilians, CNF, DKBA, KIO/KIA, KNPLF, KNPP/KA, KNU, MA, MNDAA, NA-B, PDF, PNLO, SSPP/SSA, TNLA, ULA/AA)
  • Date (month and year)
  • Location (state and region)

The dashboard does not provide individual casualty details (names, ages, etc.).

Website https://mmpeacemonitor.org/en/ongoing-dashboard/military-regimes-airstrikes-in-myanmar-1-feb-2021-current/

Nyan Lynn Thit Analytica Dashboard

Nyan Lynn Thit Analytica Dashboard The Nyan Lynn Thit Analytica Dashboard tracks conflict incidents and displays total deaths and injuries.

Filter categories

  • State/region
  • Township
  • Actor type (military, ethnic armed organization/People's Defence Force, unknown)
  • Date range
  • Attack type (combat engagement, airstrike, artillery, drone, arrest, raided and seized, landmine, column, shooting, arson, bomb, mine, assassinate, arrest and kill, unknown)

The dashboard provides aggregate casualty figures but does not provide individual casualty details (names, ages, etc.).

Website https://www.nyanlynnthitanalytica.org/dashboard

AAPP Coup Data Explorer

The Assistance Association for Political Prisoners (AAPP) Dashboard for People Killed Since the Coup tracks all people killed since the coup with daily verified updates. Users can identify airstrike fatalities and cross-reference with demographic and geographic data. The dashboard shows details for each person killed including detailed explanations of their cause of death.

Filter categories

  • Gender (male, female, LGBT, prefer not to say)
  • State/region
  • Township
  • Sector (Activist, Civilian, Education, Entertainer, Government, Military, Media, Medic, Member of Parliament, NGO, Parties, Public Service, -Religious, Resistance Group, Security Forces, UEC)
  • Age group (18-35, 36-44, 45-64, 65+, Unknown)
  • Deceased year
  • Deceased month
  • Cause of death (Unknown, Airstrike, Artillery, Bomb explosion, Execution, Health issues, Landmine, Set fire alive, Shot, Shot in head, Torture, -Vehicle ramming)

The tracker provides individual casualty details including names, ages, and detailed explanations of cause of death.

Website https://coup.aappb.org/data-explorer

Myanmar Witness

Myanmar Witness conducts open-source investigations documenting airstrikes through geolocation, satellite imagery, and user-generated content analysis. Part of the Centre for Information Resilience (CIR), they publish regular verified incident reports suitable for accountability, legal documentation, and countering junta disinformation.

Resources include

  • Filterable reports database (includes airstrike category filter)
  • Aircraft identification guides (images, capabilities, procurement, tail numbers, significant airstrike events)
  • Interactive map of Myanmar Air Force (MAF) airbases

Reports provide verified incident details but do not systematically track individual casualty information (names, ages, etc.).

Reports https://www.info-res.org/myanmar-witness/reports/

Aircraft Guides and Incidents https://www.info-res.org/myanmar-witness/guides/aircraft/

Map of MAF Airbases in Myanmar https://www.info-res.org/myanmar-witness/maps/map-of-maf-airbases-in-myanmar/

Wai Hin Scout Airstrike Alert (Early Warning App)

The Wai Hin Scout Airstrike Alert is an early warning progressive web app (PWA) published by Wai Hin Scout, a community resistance network that has been sharing aerial attack alerts via social media since 2022 using information submitted by residents across Burma. Released in February 2025, the app provides live incoming aircraft alerts. It is not a database of past airstrike events.

A user is required to select their current location, or the region, state and township they want to monitor, as well as the distance range. At a 62 mile (100 km) radius, the app alerts them that they will have about eight minutes to take shelter when an aircraft enters the selected area. Users can add the website to their device home screen to function like a mobile app.

Alert coverage

  • Burma Air Force aircraft
  • Motorized paragliders
  • Gyrocopters

The app does not track historical airstrike data, casualties or individual incident records.
