Swan Energy Company Limited
Profile 1.5
Summary Assessment
Swan Energy Company Limited is a Myanmar-registered company in the Asia Sun Group / Shoon Group network that is one of three Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers of aviation fuel documented in the UN Special Rapporteur's June 2024 Banking on the Death Trade report. The UNSR's transaction table records the three proxies, Swan Energy, Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading, as having purchased a combined total of approximately USD 15 million of aviation fuel in calendar year 2023. No per-entity breakdown is given in the report. Swan Energy and Myan-Oil purchased from CB Energy through United Amara Bank to Krung Thai Bank; Rich Ray Trading purchased from Tawan-Oil Trading through United Amara Bank to Kasikorn Bank. Swan Energy's own website states that it sells 10,000 metric tonnes of aviation fuel to Myanmar every month, which it claims constitutes 100 percent of the Myanmar aviation fuel market. This claim, cited by the UN Special Rapporteur on the situation of human rights in Myanmar, is the single most explicit self-disclosure of centrality to the supply chain made by any entity in this database. The company name derives from its reported co-owner Sein Wan, a Myanmar crony businessman with long-standing ties to the military who is related by marriage to a current junta minister. Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung, a director and former shareholder of Asia Sun Trading Company Limited who is sanctioned by the UK and Canada, is also reportedly a co-owner and represented Swan Energy in a junta-linked energy committee. Swan Energy was unsanctioned in all jurisdictions until October 2024, when Canada and the United Kingdom became the first jurisdictions to designate it. It remains unsanctioned by the United States, European Union and Australia as of April 2026.

1
Role in the Aviation Fuel Supply Chain

Swan Energy Company Limited is an Asia Sun Group / Shoon Group proxy that operates as a direct purchaser of aviation fuel from Thai trading companies and a distributor within Myanmar. It is one of three Myanmar-registered proxy entities, alongside Myan-Oil Company Limited and Rich Ray Trading Company Limited, documented by the UN Special Rapporteur as having purchased aviation fuel in calendar year 2023 as part of the Asia Sun Group's side of the supply chain. Its parent entity in the UNSR's transaction table is Asia Sun Group, distinct from NEPAS, which operated its own direct purchasing and proxy structure in parallel.

Purchasing role in 2023

The UN Special Rapporteur's June 2024 Banking on the Death Trade report documented, from confidential trade records, that Swan Energy was one of three Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers of aviation fuel in calendar year 2023. The UNSR's Figure 7 transaction table shows Swan Energy, Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading collectively under the Asia Sun Group parent entity, with a combined purchase total of approximately USD 15 million. The report does not provide a per-entity breakdown. Swan Energy and Myan-Oil are shown purchasing from CB Energy, with payment processed through United Amara Bank to CB Energy's account at Krung Thai Bank. Rich Ray Trading is shown purchasing from Tawan-Oil Trading, with payment processed through United Amara Bank to Tawan-Oil's account at Kasikorn Bank. (UNSR Jun 2024)

Junta committee representation

Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung, reportedly a co-owner of Swan Energy, represented the company in a junta-linked committee originally formed in August 2022 to import fuel from Russia, which was reorganised in October 2023 under the chairmanship of former Lieutenant General Nyo Saw, chairman of the military-owned conglomerate Myanmar Economic Corporation (MEC). This establishes a direct organisational link between Swan Energy and the junta's military-commercial apparatus at the committee level. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025)

Swan Energy's market share claim and sanctions evasion: The UN Special Rapporteur used Swan Energy's 100 percent market share claim to illustrate the failure of due diligence by the Thai banks processing the transactions. If a company publicly claims to control the entire Myanmar aviation fuel market on its corporate website, any bank handling payments to that company on behalf of Myanmar-registered entities was on notice of the likely end-use. This makes Swan Energy's transactions not merely a sanctions gap but a due diligence failure that is directly documented in the UNSR's report.
2
Evidence
UNSR transaction documentation, calendar year 2023

The UN Special Rapporteur's June 2024 report documents Swan Energy's purchasing role from confidential trade records. Figure 7 of the report lists Swan Energy, Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading as the three Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers in calendar year 2023, with a combined total of approximately USD 15 million. No per-entity breakdown is provided. Swan Energy and Myan-Oil are shown as purchasing from CB Energy (Thailand), processed through United Amara Bank to CB Energy's account at Krung Thai Bank. Rich Ray Trading is shown as purchasing from Tawan-Oil Trading (Thailand), processed through United Amara Bank to Tawan-Oil's account at Kasikorn Bank. (UNSR Jun 2024)

Market share claim from company website

The UN Special Rapporteur's report includes a screenshot (Figure 8) of Swan Energy's company website (basc.com.mm, captured 30 May 2024) showing the company's stated claim to sell 10,000 metric tonnes of aviation fuel per month and to hold 100 percent of the Myanmar aviation fuel market share. The UNSR noted: "Swan Energy's website openly states that it sells 10,000 metric tons of aviation fuel to Myanmar every month, which it claims is 100 percent of the Myanmar market share." This claim is sourced to the company itself. The profile treats this as evidence of the company's self-declared role; it is not independently verified against Port Authority import data. (UNSR Jun 2024)

UK designation basis

The UK designated Swan Energy Company Limited on 29 October 2024 on the basis that it is "associated with Asia Sun Trading Company Limited and by making available economic resources (aviation fuel) directly or indirectly to or for the benefit of the Myanmar security forces." This is the first official government designation that identifies Swan Energy as directly supplying aviation fuel to Myanmar's security forces. (UK Oct 2024)

Reported ownership and military connections

The Irrawaddy reported in January 2025 that the company name "Swan" derives from Sein Wan, a crony businessman from Mon State with long-standing ties to the Myanmar military who is related by marriage to a current junta minister. Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung, who is sanctioned by the UK (January 2023) and Canada (October 2024) as a director and former shareholder of Asia Sun Trading Company Limited, is also reportedly a co-owner of Swan Energy. Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung represented Swan Energy in a junta-linked petroleum committee chaired by former Lieutenant General Nyo Saw of MEC. These ownership details are drawn from Irrawaddy reporting and are described as reported rather than confirmed through corporate registry records. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025)

•Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung: Reportedly a co-owner of Swan Energy; confirmed as having represented Swan Energy in a junta-linked petroleum committee in October 2023. Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung is sanctioned by the UK (January 2023) and Canada (October 2024) as a director and former shareholder of Asia Sun Trading Company Limited. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025; UK Jan 2023; JFM Oct 2024)

Described by the Irrawaddy as a crony businessman from Mon State with long-standing military ties who is related by marriage to a current junta minister. Not sanctioned in any jurisdiction as of April 2026. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025)
•Myan-Oil Company Limited and Rich Ray Trading Company Limited: The other Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers documented in the UNSR's 2023 transaction table alongside Swan Energy. Both sanctioned by the UK in October 2024. See Joint Profile 1.6. (UNSR Jun 2024; JFM Oct 2024)
•Myanmar Economic Corporation (MEC) committee: Win Kyaw Kyaw Aung represented Swan Energy in a junta-linked petroleum committee chaired by former Lieutenant General Nyo Saw, chairman of MEC. MEC is sanctioned by the US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia. This committee link establishes a direct organisational connection between Swan Energy and the military's commercial apparatus. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025)

The Swan Energy sanctions gap: Swan Energy publicly claims 100 percent of the Myanmar aviation fuel market. It is documented in the UNSR's confidential trade records as a direct purchaser from a Thai supplier. It was unsanctioned in all five jurisdictions for nearly two years after the supply chain was publicly documented by Amnesty International (November 2022), and for four months after the UNSR specifically confirmed Swan Energy's purchasing role in confidential trade records (June 2024). It remains unsanctioned by the United States, European Union and Australia as of April 2026. Swan Energy falls squarely within the OFAC jet fuel sector determination of August 2023: any entity operating in the jet fuel sector of the Burmese economy is susceptible to designation. Its own website constitutes public evidence of its operating role. The UNSR's transaction documentation provides the evidentiary basis for designation submissions. (UNSR Jun 2024; OFAC Aug 2023)

Advocacy Interventions to Date
•June 2024. The UN Special Rapporteur's Banking on the Death Trade report documents Swan Energy as one of three Asia Sun Group proxy purchasers, with Swan Energy, Myan-Oil and Rich Ray Trading collectively purchasing approximately USD 15 million of aviation fuel in 2023, with no per-entity breakdown given. The report also publishes a screenshot of Swan Energy's website showing its 100 percent market share claim. The UNSR explicitly states that Swan Energy has not been sanctioned by any government and calls for holistic sanctioning of the Asia Sun network. No designation follows immediately. (UNSR Jun 2024)
•October 2024. Canada and the United Kingdom become the first jurisdictions to sanction Swan Energy Company Limited. The UK designates it for being associated with Asia Sun Trading Company Limited and for making available aviation fuel to Myanmar's security forces. Justice for Myanmar welcomes the designations. Swan Energy remains unsanctioned by the United States, European Union and Australia. (UK Oct 2024; JFM Oct 2024)
•February 2026. Justice for Myanmar calls for coordinated sanctions on vessels, their owners and managers, Hai Linh, and MPE. Swan Energy is not named explicitly in this press release. It remains unsanctioned by the US, EU and Australia as of April 2026. (JFM Feb 2026)
6
Recommended Actions
•Designate Swan Energy in the US, EU and Australia: Swan Energy's own website constitutes public evidence of operating in the jet fuel sector of the Burmese economy within the meaning of the August 2023 OFAC sector determination under Executive Order 14014. The UNSR's documented role of Swan Energy as a proxy purchaser of aviation fuel, as set out in Figure 7 of the Banking on the Death Trade report, provides the evidentiary basis for a formal designation submission to OFAC. The same evidence supports EU and Australian submissions. The UK and Canada have already acted; the remaining three jurisdictions should follow without delay. (UNSR Jun 2024; OFAC Aug 2023)
•Designate Sein Wan in all five jurisdictions: Sein Wan is the reported namesake and co-owner of Swan Energy and has long-standing military ties; he is related by marriage to a current junta minister. He has not been sanctioned by any jurisdiction as of April 2026. Civil society should request that sanctions authorities investigate Sein Wan's ownership stake and connections to Swan Energy's aviation fuel operations as grounds for individual designation. (Irrawaddy Jan 2025)
•Use Swan Energy's market share claim in due diligence engagement with CB Energy and Krung Thai Bank: The publicly available market share claim on Swan Energy's website (basc.com.mm) means that any counterparty transacting with Swan Energy had access to information establishing its centrality to Myanmar's aviation fuel supply chain. Civil society should formally notify CB Energy and Krung Thai Bank of the UK and Canada designations of Swan Energy and the evidentiary record in the UNSR report, requesting that they confirm they have ceased all transactions with Swan Energy and entities in the Asia Sun Group network. The UNSR's report provides a template for this engagement. (UNSR Jun 2024)
