Notes
Offloading terminal naming across reports

The physical terminal at Thilawa, Yangon has been referred to by different names across source reports, reflecting successive changes in ownership and management.

Rows 1 to 8PEAS terminal, under Puma Energy control.
Rows 9 to 10Former PEAS terminal, still under Puma Energy control at time of delivery. Sale to Shoon Energy completed 25 December 2022.
Rows 11 to 20Former PEAS terminal, under Shoon Energy / Myanma Petrochemical Enterprise (MPE) joint management.
Rows 21 to 30Myan Oil Terminal Company Limited, the terminal's current legal name as used in Amnesty International (January 2026) and Reuters (January 2026).
Volume units

Volume figures use the units reported in the original source. Amnesty International (November 2022) reported volumes in metric tonnes (MT). Amnesty International (January 2024) reported volumes in kilotonne (kt). Amnesty International (July 2024) reported values in approximate USD rather than volume. Rows 29 and 30 are reported in MT per Amnesty International (January 2026) and Reuters (January 2026).
