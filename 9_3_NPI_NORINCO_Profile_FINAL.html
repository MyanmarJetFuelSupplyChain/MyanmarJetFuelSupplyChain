North Petroleum International Co. Ltd. and China North Industries Group Corporation Limited (NORINCO)
Profile 9.3
Summary Assessment
North Petroleum International Co. Ltd. is a subsidiary of China ZhenHua Oil Co. Ltd., itself a subsidiary of NORINCO (China North Industries Group Corporation), a Chinese state-owned arms conglomerate. North Petroleum holds the 40 percent stake in Goldpetrol Joint Operating Company Inc. not held by Interra Resources, making it a co-owner of the oil extraction operation whose output feeds Myanmar's domestic crude-to-aviation-fuel chain. NORINCO separately supplies weapons directly to the Myanmar military and operates copper mines in Myanmar with MEHL, the junta's primary military conglomerate. North Petroleum was also a major Interra Resources shareholder until at least September 2023. Neither North Petroleum nor China ZhenHua Oil has been designated in connection with Myanmar. NORINCO appears on the US DoD 1260H list and the US OFAC NS-CMIC list under authorities unrelated to Myanmar, and on Japan's METI End User List. Principal advocacy actions relating to Goldpetrol are in Profile 9.1; MOGE actions are in Profile 9.2.

1
Corporate Structure and Connection to the Myanmar Supply Chain

North Petroleum International holds 40 percent of Goldpetrol through the China ZhenHua Oil group. All crude oil extracted by Goldpetrol from the Chauk and Yenangyaung fields is sold to MOGE, which transfers it to MPE for refining at Mann Thanbayarkan into petroleum products including aviation fuel. NORINCO as North Petroleum's ultimate parent is therefore an indirect co-owner of the upstream crude supply chain for Myanmar's domestic aviation fuel production. At the same time, NORINCO is a direct arms supplier to the Myanmar military, giving it a dual role as weapons supplier and fuel chain co-owner. (JFM Jan 2025)

North Petroleum International was additionally a major Interra Resources shareholder on the Singapore Exchange until at least September 2023, creating a direct financial interest in the listed company whose Goldpetrol operations are the subject of the JFM/Finance Uncovered investigation and the ongoing SGX RegCo review. The full extent of North Petroleum's current Interra shareholding has not been confirmed in any reviewed source since September 2023. (JFM Jan 2025)

NORINCO was listed on the US Department of Defense list of Chinese Military Companies under Section 1260H of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2021, with a listing date of 3 June 2021. NORINCO was subsequently placed on the US OFAC Non-SDN Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Companies (NS-CMIC) list effective 2 August 2021, under Executive Order 13959 as amended by Executive Order 14032, which prohibits US persons from purchasing or selling NORINCO's publicly traded securities. As a consequence of the NS-CMIC listing, NORINCO is automatically excluded from US federal procurement under the SAM Procurement Exclusions system (which operates on a reciprocal basis with OFAC restrictions) and appears on the US Trade Consolidated Screening List (CSL). NORINCO is also listed on Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) End User List. None of these designations are Myanmar-specific. NORINCO has not been designated by the EU, UK, Canada or Australia in connection with either Myanmar or China defence sector concerns as of April 2026.

North Petroleum International Co. Ltd. and China ZhenHua Oil Co. Ltd. have not been independently designated by any government as of April 2026. No designation specific to their co-ownership of Goldpetrol or their role in the Myanmar crude supply chain has been identified in any reviewed sanctions list.

3
Key Relationships
•Goldpetrol Joint Operating Company Inc. / Interra Resources Ltd (Profile 9.1): North Petroleum International holds 40 percent of Goldpetrol. All crude oil produced by Goldpetrol from the Chauk and Yenangyaung fields is sold to MOGE under the Improved Petroleum Recovery Contracts. North Petroleum International's co-ownership position means it benefits financially from Goldpetrol's revenues, which flow through the same payment structure that has been documented as generating more than USD 200 million in oil sales to MOGE between January 2021 and the end of 2024. (JFM Jan 2025)
•Myanma Oil and Gas Enterprise (MOGE) (Profile 9.2): As Goldpetrol's co-owner, North Petroleum International is indirectly party to the IPRC relationship with MOGE, the junta-controlled state oil enterprise that is the mandatory purchaser of all Goldpetrol crude. MOGE is designated by the EU (21 February 2022), subject to a US financial services directive (effective 15 December 2023), and designated by Switzerland (11 March 2025). (Profile 9.2; Open source, March 2025)
•Myanma Economic Holdings Limited (MEHL): NORINCO separately operates copper mines in Myanmar in partnership with MEHL, the junta's primary military conglomerate. MEHL is designated by the US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia. This separate NORINCO-MEHL relationship runs alongside the North Petroleum International-Goldpetrol relationship and is part of the broader pattern of NORINCO's military and commercial engagement with the Myanmar junta. (JFM Jan 2025)
•Yang Peilin / Heilongjiang United Oil and Chemicals Co. Ltd. (HUOC): Yang Peilin, Interra Resources' second-largest shareholder at 5.7 percent as of January 2025, is the chairperson of Heilongjiang United Oil and Chemicals Co. Ltd. (HUOC), a Chinese commodity trading firm that imports crude oil from Russia and Southeast Asia. HUOC signed a strategic cooperation agreement with China ZhenHua Oil in 2015 to 2016, creating a documented commercial link between the Interra shareholder register and the NORINCO oil trading arm. (JFM Jan 2025)

Advocacy Interventions to Date
•29 January 2025. Justice for Myanmar and Finance Uncovered identify the NORINCO chain as the ultimate beneficial owner of the 40 percent North Petroleum stake in Goldpetrol and document NORINCO's dual role as Myanmar arms supplier and Goldpetrol co-owner. NORINCO does not respond. (JFM Jan 2025)
•12 February 2025. JFM's rebuttal of Interra Resources' SGX response reiterates the significance of the NORINCO chain. No response from NORINCO or North Petroleum follows. (JFM Feb 2025)
5
Recommended Actions
•Submit Myanmar-specific designation evidence for NORINCO and North Petroleum International. Existing US listings (DoD 1260H and NS-CMIC) impose only limited financial restrictions and do not create Myanmar sanctions exposure. OFAC designation under the Burma EO and the August 2023 sector determination would extend to NORINCO's involvement in Myanmar's jet fuel sector through its indirect co-ownership of Goldpetrol. The evidentiary chain from Goldpetrol crude to Mann refining to military base supply, documented in AI Nov 2022 and JFM Jan 2025, provides the basis. UK, EU, Canada, and Australia submissions should be filed simultaneously. (JFM Jan 2025; OFAC Aug 2023)
•Raise NORINCO's dual role diplomatically with China. Governments engaging China on Myanmar should raise NORINCO's simultaneous role as weapons supplier and Goldpetrol co-owner. This dual connection is the clearest documented case of a single Chinese state entity spanning both the weapons supply chain and the domestic fuel chain sustaining the Myanmar military's air campaign. (JFM Jan 2025)
•Investigate North Petroleum's current Interra shareholding and submit to SGX RegCo. North Petroleum's shareholding in Interra had not been publicly confirmed since September 2023. If North Petroleum retains a significant stake, that connection should be part of SGX RegCo's review of Interra's trading resumption. Civil society should formally request that SGX RegCo disclose whether North Petroleum's current shareholding has been addressed as part of the review and whether NORINCO's weapons supply role was assessed in Interra's legal opinion. (JFM Jan 2025; JFM May 2025)
