Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA and Sea Hill Maritime SA
Profile 6.8
Summary Assessment
Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA and Sea Hill Maritime SA are UAE entities registered in the Hamriyah Free Zone, Sharjah, that are the registered owners of REEF and NOBLE respectively. These two vessels carried all eight documented Iranian aviation fuel deliveries to Myanmar between October 2024 and December 2025. Both entities were first named publicly in Justice for Myanmar's February 2026 press release. Neither appeared in any reviewed source during the period of deliveries. Both share their Hamriyah Free Zone address with Sea Route Ship Management FZE, the ISM and commercial manager of both vessels, which has been OFAC-designated since June 2024. The co-location of the owners and manager of two simultaneously US-sanctioned ghost fleet vessels at a single address is a strong basis for investigation into common beneficial ownership. Neither Amethyst nor Sea Hill has been sanctioned by any government as of April 2026.

Connection to the Myanmar Aviation Fuel Supply Chain

Both entities connect to the Myanmar supply chain exclusively as registered owners of REEF and NOBLE. Together those two vessels account for all eight documented Iranian aviation fuel deliveries to Myanmar between October 2024 and December 2025. Neither Amethyst nor Sea Hill was identified in any reviewed source during the delivery period. Both were named for the first time in JFM's February 2026 press release. The late identification, more than a year after the first delivery, reflects the difficulty of tracing vessel ownership through free zone structures and underscores the importance of beneficial ownership disclosure requirements. 3
Co-location and Structural Analysis

Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA, Sea Hill Maritime SA, and Sea Route Ship Management FZE all share a Hamriyah Free Zone address in Sharjah, UAE. Sea Route manages both REEF and NOBLE commercially and under the ISM Code, sitting as the operational layer above both registered owners. The concentration of the manager and registered owners of two simultaneously US-sanctioned ghost fleet vessels at a single shared address is consistent with shadow fleet practice and makes beneficial ownership tracing substantially more difficult. No reviewed source has confirmed a common controlling party, but any designation submissions should address this structural connection explicitly. (JFM Feb 2026)

5
Recommended Actions
•Designate Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA and Sea Hill Maritime SA across all five jurisdictions. Both are registered owners of US-sanctioned ghost fleet vessels that continued delivering aviation fuel to Myanmar after their June 2024 OFAC designations. The owners were not designated alongside the vessels, creating the structural gap that has sustained ongoing operations. JFM's February 2026 call for coordinated designation across all five jurisdictions remains unimplemented. Designation of both owners alongside Sea Route Ship Management FZE would close the ownership layer of the ghost fleet corporate structure. If investigation establishes common beneficial ownership, a network designation naming all three co-located entities under common control is the priority. (JFM Feb 2026)
•Engage UAE authorities to compel beneficial ownership disclosure for all three co-located Hamriyah Free Zone entities. UAE Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 on beneficial ownership registers provides the domestic legal basis. Engagement should target the Hamriyah Free Zone Authority, the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit, and the Executive Office of AML/CFT. The UAE's 2025 AML Law (in force from 14 October 2025) strengthens the domestic framework further. The UAE has a FATF mutual evaluation scheduled for 2026, creating additional institutional leverage for advocacy partners pressing for enforcement action against co-located entities engaged in documented sanctions evasion. (JFM Feb 2026)
•Seek Myanmar-specific designation of REEF under UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian sanctions alongside designation of Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA. REEF was designated by OFAC under Iran sanctions but not under Myanmar-specific instruments. UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian Myanmar sanctions instruments address aviation fuel supply and military support. Myanmar-specific designation would provide a stronger basis for port and insurance denials in non-US jurisdictions not bound by Iran sanctions programmes. (JFM Feb 2026; AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
