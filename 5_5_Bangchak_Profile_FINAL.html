Bangchak Corporation Public Company Limited
Profile 5.5
Summary Assessment
Bangchak Corporation Public Company Limited is a Thai integrated energy company listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET: BCP). It owns and operates the Bangchak Phra Khanong Refinery at Bangkok Port, which was identified by Amnesty International, Global Witness and Burma Campaign UK in their March 2023 investigation as the load port for the October 2022 BIG SEA 104 shipment of aviation fuel to Myanmar (12,592 MT). That shipment arrived at the former PEAS terminal at Thilawa, Yangon, while Puma Energy retained control of the terminal pending completion of its sale to the Shoon Group in December 2022. Bangchak did not respond to Amnesty International's letters. Bangchak does not appear in any subsequent investigated supply chain. Bangchak Corporation Public Company Limited has not been sanctioned by any government.

1
Role in the Aviation Fuel Supply Chain

Bangchak Corporation Public Company Limited is identified in the March 2023 joint investigation by Amnesty International, Global Witness and Burma Campaign UK as the owner of the Bangchak Oil Refinery at Bangkok Port, Thailand, which was the load port for the October 2022 BIG SEA 104 shipment of Jet A-1 to Myanmar. BIG SEA 104 (IMO 9635535) departed the Bangchak Oil Refinery on or about 8 October 2022 and arrived at the former PEAS terminal at Thilawa, Yangon, on approximately 15 October 2022, offloading 12,592 MT of Jet A-1. (AI Mar 2023)

The October 2022 shipment took place during the brief transition period after Puma Energy announced its Myanmar exit on 5 October 2022 but before the sale to the Shoon Group was completed in December 2022. At the time of the BIG SEA 104 delivery, the Thilawa terminal therefore remained under Puma Energy's control. The BIG SEA 104 was beneficially owned by Prima Marine Plc, a Thai company, and was registered for Protection and Indemnity insurance with the Shipowners' P&I Club (Luxembourg). Neither Bangchak, Prima Marine nor Shipowners' Club responded to Amnesty International's contact. (AI Mar 2023)

Key Relationships
•BIG SEA 104 (IMO 9635535): The vessel that transported the October 2022 shipment from the Bangchak Oil Refinery, Bangkok Port, to the Thilawa terminal in Yangon. Departed on or about 8 October 2022, arrived approximately 15 October 2022, offloaded 12,592 MT of Jet A-1. Beneficially owned by Prima Marine Plc, a Thai company. See Profile 6.11. (AI Mar 2023)
•Prima Marine Plc (beneficial owner of BIG SEA 104): A Thai company that was the beneficial owner of BIG SEA 104 at the time of the October 2022 shipment. Its ownership of the vessel makes it a direct co-participant in the documented shipment alongside Bangchak as the refinery owner. Prima Marine Plc did not respond to Amnesty International's letters. (AI Mar 2023)
•The Shipowners' Mutual Protection and Indemnity Association (Luxembourg) ("Shipowners' P&I Club"): Provided protection and indemnity insurance for BIG SEA 104 at the time of the October 2022 shipment. P&I insurance is a prerequisite for commercial vessel operation and its provision constitutes a material enabling service for the shipment. The Shipowners' P&I Club did not respond to Amnesty International's letters. (AI Mar 2023)
•Myan Oil Terminal Company Limited / former PEAS terminal (Thilawa, Yangon): The destination terminal to which the October 2022 BIG SEA 104 shipment was delivered. At the time of the delivery the terminal remained under Puma Energy's control, pending the completion of the sale to the Shoon Group in December 2022. The terminal operating company itself has not been sanctioned in any jurisdiction. Its direct owner, P.E.I. Energy Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), is sanctioned by the US. Its parent group entity Shoon Energy Pte. Ltd. and the beneficial owner of the ownership chain, Khin Phyu Win, are sanctioned by the US, UK and Canada. See Profile 1.3 and Joint Profile 2.1. (AI Mar 2023)
•Puma Energy Holdings Pte. Ltd.: Puma Energy announced its Myanmar exit on 5 October 2022 and completed the sale in December 2022. The Bangchak-origin October 2022 shipment was one of two shipments (the other being the December 2022 PRIME V shipment from Reliance Industries, row 10) that arrived at the Thilawa terminal during this post-announcement, pre-completion transition period. See Profile 5.1. (AI Mar 2023)

4
Advocacy Interventions to Date
•March 2023. Amnesty International, Global Witness and Burma Campaign UK publish their investigation into new aviation fuel shipments to Myanmar, identifying the Bangchak Oil Refinery at Bangkok Port as the load port for the October 2022 BIG SEA 104 shipment. Amnesty International contacted Bangchak by letter; no response was received. No further advocacy action targeting Bangchak has been documented as of April 2026. (AI Mar 2023)
5
Recommended Actions
•Seek clarification of Bangchak's commercial role in the October 2022 transaction: The source record establishes that Bangchak owns the refinery that served as the load port for the BIG SEA 104 shipment but does not confirm whether Bangchak was the direct seller of the Jet A-1. Civil society should engage Bangchak directly, requesting disclosure of: the commercial structure of the October 2022 transaction, including the identity of the purchaser of the fuel at the Bangchak refinery; what end-use due diligence Bangchak conducted on the purchaser; and what policies Bangchak now applies to sales of jet fuel where the purchaser may transport it to Myanmar or to entities associated with the Myanmar military. As a SET-listed company with a Singapore-incorporated trading arm (BCP Trading Pte. Ltd.), Bangchak has disclosure obligations to shareholders and is subject to engagement by institutional investors. (AI Mar 2023)
•Monitor for any future Bangchak refinery involvement in Myanmar-bound aviation fuel supply chains: Bangchak's documented connection is limited to the single October 2022 shipment. The company does not appear in any reviewed investigation after AI Mar 2023. However, Bangchak's Phra Khanong refinery produces jet fuel and has a Singapore trading arm positioned to engage in regional petroleum product transactions. If any future Myanmar-bound shipment is traced to a Bangchak refinery or to BCP Trading Pte. Ltd., that finding should trigger immediate re-engagement and formal disclosure requests. (AI Mar 2023)
