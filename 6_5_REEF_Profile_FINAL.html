REEF (formerly BALTIC HORIZON / PETRA)
Profile 6.5
Summary Assessment
REEF (IMO 9263382), formerly PETRA and BALTIC HORIZON, is a Guinea-flagged tanker that made four confirmed aviation fuel deliveries to Myanmar between October 2024 and December 2025, loading at Bandar Abbas, Iran while broadcasting falsified AIS positions. OFAC designated REEF (then BALTIC HORIZON) on 27 June 2024 under Iran sanctions, four months before its first documented Myanmar delivery. It continued operating after designation, delivering more than 31,000 metric tonnes of confirmed cargo in its two largest deliveries alone. Satellite imagery confirmed it at Bandar Abbas while its AIS simultaneously placed it near Iraq's Basrah Oil Terminal. The day after its December 2025 delivery, Myanmar's military bombed a 300-bed hospital in Mrauk-U, killing at least 30 people. Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA (UAE) is the registered owner; Sea Route Ship Management FZE (UAE), also OFAC-designated, is the ISM and commercial manager. The UK, EU, Canada, and Australia have not designated REEF.

2
Iranian Fuel Origin and AIS Evasion

Kpler assessed all four Myanmar deliveries as originating from Iran. Satellite imagery confirmed REEF at Bandar Abbas before the October and December 2025 deliveries while its AIS displayed box-shaped loitering patterns over 750 kilometres away. For the September to October 2025 delivery cycle, Reuters documented the spoofing in precise detail: at 07:28 on 16 September, REEF's AIS showed it approximately 330 kilometres from Bandar Abbas; three minutes later the same signal showed it at port, a journey requiring at least ten hours. After loading, it transited the Strait of Hormuz with accurate AIS briefly before spoofing again, showing itself heading to Chittagong, Bangladesh, while its real position was at the Myan Oil Terminal in Yangon. The December 2025 delivery followed the same pattern. Myanmar Witness independently confirmed REEF's Myan Oil Terminal arrivals. As of late January 2026, SynMax data showed REEF loading again and heading toward Yangon. (AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)

3
Prior Sanctions History and Iran Shadow Fleet Context

REEF was designated by OFAC on 27 June 2024 as BALTIC HORIZON under Executive Order 13846 for transporting sanctioned Iranian petrochemical products, alongside Sea Route Ship Management FZE and NOBLE. The designation preceded its first Myanmar delivery by four months. The vessel was then renamed REEF and reflagged to Guinea. It made four more Myanmar deliveries after designation. The continued deliveries by a US-sanctioned vessel demonstrate both the limits of single-jurisdiction designation without complementary measures and the indispensability of REEF and NOBLE to Myanmar's current fuel supply. (AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)

7
Recommended Actions
•Urgently designate REEF across the UK, EU, Canada, and Australia. REEF made four Myanmar deliveries after its OFAC designation, delivering more than 31,000 confirmed metric tonnes in its two largest alone. Single-jurisdiction designation is easily evaded through flag changes and access to non-US financial and insurance services. JFM's February 2026 call for coordinated designation by the remaining four jurisdictions remains unimplemented. (JFM Feb 2026; AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
•Designate Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA across all jurisdictions. Sea Route Ship Management FZE was designated by OFAC alongside REEF but Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA, the registered owner, has not been designated by any government. Amethyst shares a Hamriyah Free Zone address with Sea Hill Maritime SA, the owner of NOBLE, suggesting a common beneficial owner. Any designation action should assess whether the two entities are connected and seek to designate both beneficial owners simultaneously. (JFM Feb 2026)
•Engage UAE authorities regarding the Hamriyah Free Zone entities. Sea Route Ship Management FZE, Amethyst Maritime Ventures SA, and Sea Hill Maritime SA all share a Hamriyah Free Zone address, placing UAE authorities in jurisdiction over the manager and both owners of REEF and NOBLE simultaneously. The Hamriyah Free Zone Authority and the UAE Executive Office of AML/CFT should be formally engaged. The UAE's 2025 AML Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025), which entered into force on 14 October 2025, expanded sanctions-evasion offences and lowered the evidentiary threshold for AML liability. (JFM Feb 2026; AI Jan 2026)
•Engage Guinea's maritime registry and raise Iran's Myanmar fuel exports diplomatically. Guinea should be formally notified of REEF's documented history as a US-sanctioned vessel. Governments engaged with Iran on nuclear negotiations or broader trade should include Myanmar aviation fuel exports explicitly in bilateral engagement, alongside pressure on IRGC-linked entities confirmed by Reuters as dominating Iran's fuel-smuggling networks. (AI Jan 2026; Reuters Jan 2026)
